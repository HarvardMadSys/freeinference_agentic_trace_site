# Spec Delta

## Purpose

The Paper figures page no longer exists as a page: its figures are sections
of the Explore page.

## REMOVED Requirements

### Requirement: The figures
**Reason**: The figures move to the Explore page, with a changed list.
**Migration**: See `explore-page`, requirement "Paper figures".

### Requirement: Same definitions as the paper
**Reason**: Moved to `explore-page` without the scope clause.
**Migration**: See `explore-page`, requirement "Same definitions as the paper".

### Requirement: Thin data is not drawn
**Reason**: Moved to `explore-page` unchanged.
**Migration**: See `explore-page`, requirement "Thin data is not drawn".

### Requirement: Figures redraw on selection change
**Reason**: Moved to `explore-page`, where drawing on scroll becomes a rule.
**Migration**: See `explore-page`, requirement "Figures draw when in view and redraw on selection change".

### Requirement: Readable in both colour schemes and on a phone
**Reason**: Moved to `explore-page` unchanged.
**Migration**: See `explore-page`, requirement "Readable in both colour schemes and on a phone".
